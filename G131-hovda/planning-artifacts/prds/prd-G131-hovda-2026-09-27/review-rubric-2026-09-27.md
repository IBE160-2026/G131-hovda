# PRD Quality Review — Studievenn

Calibration: solo student project (IBE160). The only user is the developer. The first version is in use in October 2026 and delivery is 22 Nov 2026. Rigor is judged at that level, not as an enterprise launch. The PRD also feeds architecture, epics and stories, so done-ness and downstream usability are weighted most heavily.

## Overall verdict
This is a strong PRD for its stakes. It has a clear thesis ("the app decides tonight's session so a tired student can still finish it"), a real persona, a sharp glossary, specific NFRs, and counter-metrics that show genuine thinking. The [Okt]/[Nov] staging is an honest scope decision. The risk sits in the study-plan logic. FR-9's catch-up rule is underspecified, and it collides with the fact that notes arrive lecture by lecture, which could make the "bak planen" state and its daily push notifications fire for most of the semester. The PRD also never says what happens when AI generation fails. Fix those three before architecture. The remaining findings can be handled while stories are written.

## Decision-readiness — adequate
Decisions are stated as decisions, not as considerations. Examples: 80 % as the threshold for "gjennomført" (FR-15), weekdays only for planning with weekends as "fleksibel dag" (§3), notes as the only source material (§4.3, §7), and explanation questions that do not affect completion (§4.7). The Åpne spørsmål (§11) are genuinely open, and each one names who decides and how (for example Q2 goes to architecture, with named criteria). Trade-offs are mostly implicit: §9.2 gives a reason for each exclusion ("Flashcards, fordi quizen dekker behovet"), which is good.

What is missing is any acknowledgement of the one real product tension in the design. The plan is driven by the exam date, but content is driven by lecture pace. There are also no `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts at all. That is acceptable for a solo PRD, but it means this tension is never flagged.

### Findings
- **[high]** Plan pace and note pace conflict (§4.4 FR-9, FR-23, UJ-4 "Når det går galt") — FR-9 spreads all remaining sub-chapters evenly from today to the exam, but notes are added "ett kapittel om gangen utover semesteret" (§4.3, UJ-2). If lectures run slower than an even spread, the user is "bak" whenever the plan reaches sub-chapters that have not been lectured yet. She then gets a push every day including weekends (FR-23), which is exactly the SM-C2 failure ("Blir varslene masete, slår brukeren dem av"). UJ-4 handles missing notes for *today's suggestion*, but not for the *behind calculation* or the notifications. *Fix:* Decide explicitly, and state it in FR-9, whether sub-chapters without notes count toward "bak". Options: (a) only sub-chapters with notes are planned and counted; (b) keep the full plan, but suppress FR-23 when the deficit consists only of sub-chapters without notes; (c) let the user set an expected note date per chapter. Add a `[NOTE FOR PM]` if this stays undecided.
- **[medium]** No cut line for the October version (§9.1) — [Okt] contains 14 FRs (account, parsing, AI translation, AI summary, AI question bank, quiz, error reports, progress, plus deployment through NFR-7) with about 4 weeks left and one developer. The PRD does not say which of them can slip to November if October gets tight. *Fix:* Name a minimal usable core for October (for example FR-1, FR-3, FR-4, FR-8, FR-11, FR-13, FR-14, FR-15, FR-20). Mark FR-5, FR-12 and FR-16 as "Okt if possible, otherwise Nov".

## Substance over theater — strong
There is one persona, Marte, and she drives concrete decisions: evening time slot → pre-generated content (FR-8, NFR-3); tired → one tap to start (§6); interruptions → resume (FR-10); children sick → catch-up and weekend days (UJ-4). The Vision (§1) could not be swapped into another PRD: it quotes the actual daily prompt and admits that the main value is the two-in-one learning effect for IBE160 and IBE430. The NFRs have numbers (3 s, 20 s, 100 kr/month, 30 days, EU/EØS). §2.2 and §8 are candid about competitors instead of claiming novelty. Nothing reads as furniture. The one soft spot is §6 (see Done-ness).

## Strategic coherence — strong
The thesis is clear and carried through: removing the decision about what to do, plus honest feedback against the learning outcomes, turns a short tired evening into completed progress. The features line up behind it. The Okt/Nov split is thesis-first: the October version delivers the core evening loop (suggestion → summary → quiz → check off), and November adds planning, assessment and notifications. The split does not simply put the easiest work first. The success metrics test the thesis (sessions per week, 90 % completion, realism of the assessment). Counter-metrics SM-C1–C3 are well chosen and each maps to a specific SM.

### Findings
- **[medium]** Student SMs cannot be measured by the IBE160 deadline (§10 SM-2, SM-3) — Both are measured "fram til eksamen i IBE430 i desember 2026", after delivery on 22 Nov. The IBE160 report can only show SM-4 and SM-5 plus partial SM-1. *Fix:* Add interim checkpoints measurable on 22 Nov, for example "on 22 Nov: in line with or ahead of the study plan" and "share of assessments the developer judges accurate in a weekly self-check". Keep the December measures as the final validation.
- **[low]** SM attribution is overclaimed (§10 SM-1 "Viser at FR-10 og FR-22 virker") — FR-22 does not exist in October, and usage alone cannot isolate which FR caused it. *Fix:* Change it to "Indikerer at…", or split the measurement into before and after FR-22.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate
Most FRs have concrete, testable consequences. Strong examples: FR-4 (IBE430 list → exactly 6 chapters and 21 sub-chapters), FR-14 (10 multiple-choice questions, 4 options, "8 av 10"), FR-15 (80 %), FR-1 (30 days), FR-22 (default 21:15). The weak spots cluster where downstream work leans hardest: the planning algorithm, AI failure paths, and the definition of an "økt".

### Findings
- **[high]** FR-9 catch-up and "i rute" rules are underspecified (§4.4 FR-9) — "fordeler delkapitlene jevnt", "øker planen antallet delkapitler per dag" and "til brukeren er i rute igjen" leave open how fractional per-day targets are rounded, how large the increase is, whether weekends are used until the deficit is zero or with some tolerance, and how "hvor mye" (FR-20) is expressed. This is core logic that NFR-8 requires automated tests for, and it drives FR-10, FR-20, FR-23 and SM-2. *Fix:* State the rule precisely. For example: target(today) = ceil(total_remaining_at_plan_start × elapsed_study_days / total_study_days); "bak" = completed < target(today); when behind, spread the remaining sub-chapters over the remaining study days plus flexible days; "i rute" = completed ≥ target(today); the deficit is shown as a number of sub-chapters.
- **[high]** No behavior when AI generation fails or is slow (FR-5, FR-8, FR-11, FR-13, FR-19, FR-21; NFR-3) — NFR-3's "under 3 sekunder" depends on content being generated when notes are saved (FR-8), but nothing says what happens if that generation fails, is still running, or hits the cost cap (NFR-6). If this is not specified, the evening flow in UJ-1 can dead-end. *Fix:* Add consequences to FR-8, for example: notes are saved even if generation fails; the sub-chapter shows "innhold lages" or "feilet – prøv igjen"; generation retries automatically; FR-10 does not suggest a sub-chapter whose content is not ready. Add one line to FR-19 and FR-21 for the error case.
- **[medium]** "Økt" has no start/end definition (§3 Økt; FR-10, FR-17, FR-21, SM-1) — FR-21 triggers "etter hver økt", FR-17 says "hver andre eller tredje økt", FR-10 resumes an interrupted økt, and SM-1 counts økter. It is unclear whether an økt ends when the quiz is submitted or when the optional explanation question is done, whether an abandoned økt counts, and whether a self-started session (FR-10 last bullet) or FR-18 practice counts. *Fix:* Extend the glossary entry: an økt starts when the user opens the summary or quiz from the suggestion or a self-selected sub-chapter, and ends when the quiz result is shown (and the explanation question is answered or skipped). Say whether FR-18 sessions count.
- **[medium]** "Svakt tema" and repetition size are undefined (§3, FR-10 Nov bullet, FR-21, UJ-4 "uten at planen forsinkes") — It is not said how a theme becomes weak, or how much repetition is added without delaying the plan. *Fix:* Define it, for example "a theme with wrong answers in ≥2 of the last 3 questions on it, or named in the latest vurdering". Cap repetition, for example at 5 questions per session, added on top of the planned sub-chapter.
- **[medium]** FR-4 parsing: AI or deterministic, and in what input format? (FR-4, NFR-8, addendum) — NFR-8 requires automated tests of "tolking av kapittellister", which is only reliable if parsing is deterministic. The addendum stores the Norwegian translated list, not the raw English paste that FR-4's acceptance test needs. *Fix:* State that the structure is parsed deterministically from numbering (1, 1.1, …) and that AI is used only for translation (FR-5). Add the exact English input text to the addendum as the test fixture.
- **[medium]** "Fortsetter der hun slapp" is not defined (FR-10 bullet 4, UJ-1) — It is unclear whether this means the same step, the same quiz with its answered questions kept, or just the same sub-chapter. *Fix:* Specify the saved state, for example "the quiz in progress with answers so far; the reading position in the summary is not saved".
- **[medium]** Vague quantities in AI FRs (FR-11 "kan leses på 5 minutter", FR-13 "omtrent 20", "begynner å gå tom", FR-17 "omtrent i hver andre eller tredje økt") — These cannot be tested as written. *Fix:* Give bounds: summary ≤ 600 words; bank = 20 questions at generation, topped up when fewer than 10 unanswered remain; explanation question with probability ~40 % per økt, or "never two økter in a row, at least every third".
- **[low]** Quiz denominator after an error report (FR-16 "teller ikke med i quizresultatet") — Is it 8 of 9, and does 80 % then apply to 9? *Fix:* State that the result and the 80 % threshold use the remaining questions as the denominator.
- **[low]** FR-6 consequence is not observable ("bruker læringsutbyttet til emnet de hører til") — *Fix:* Make it observable, for example "feedback and assessment name at least one learning outcome by its wording".
- **[low]** §6 uses adjectives, not bounds ("oversiktlig, imøtekommende og fargerik", "se noe nytt hver gang") — This is acceptable for UX direction, but "noe nytt hver gang" should be tied to specific elements: the suggestion, the indicator and the latest assessment are already listed, so say that those are what count as "new". "Ett trykk" is good.

## Scope honesty — strong
§8 (non-goals) and §9.2 (excluded, with reasons) do real work. Staging tags ([Okt], [Nov], Bør, Strekkmål) make de-scoping explicit, including split FRs (FR-10, FR-20). §4.3 names file upload as out of scope where it would otherwise be assumed. §0 says all assumptions were confirmed on 27.09, so the absence of `[ASSUMPTION]` tags is consistent. Open-item density (4 open questions, 0 notes) is low and fits the stakes.

### Findings
- **[medium]** Course and account lifecycle operations are silently absent (FR-3, FR-1) — Editing the exam date or course details, deleting a course, and password reset are not specified and not listed as non-goals. A changed exam date directly changes FR-9. *Fix:* Add "brukeren kan endre eksamensdato; studieplanen beregnes på nytt" to FR-3. Either add password reset or list it as a non-goal.
- **[low]** Offline and poor-network use is not addressed (NFR-1, UJ-1 on iPad) — This is probably a non-goal. *Fix:* Add one line to §9.2.

## Downstream usability — strong
The glossary (§3) is excellent: 27 terms, each defined once and used consistently (delkapittel, gjennomført delkapittel, quizresultat, vurdering vs tilbakemelding are kept distinct). IDs are contiguous and unique (FR-1–24, NFR-1–8, UJ-1–4, SM-1–5, SM-C1–3), and every cross-reference resolves. Each §4 subsection names the UJ it realizes. All UJs have the named protagonist Marte with inline context.

### Findings
- **[medium]** UJ-1 mixes October and November capabilities (§2.3 UJ-1) — The journey is set "tirsdag … i oktober", but shows "om hun ligger foran eller bak planen" (FR-20 Nov) and "Den oppdaterte vurderingen" (FR-21 Nov). A story writer extracting the October epic could pull these into October. *Fix:* Mark UJ-1 as describing the November version, or add a one-line October variant ("i oktober viser indikatoren bare andel gjennomført").
- **[low]** Eksamensform is captured but nothing consumes it (§3, FR-6) — The explanation-question style is hard-coded as oral exam. *Fix:* State what eksamensform drives (for example the style of explanation questions and feedback), or note that it is stored for later use.
- **[low]** FR-22 and FR-23 overlap on weekdays when behind — could she get two pushes? *Fix:* Say that on a weekday when she is behind, one combined notification is sent.

## Shape fit — strong
This is a single-operator, solo, chain-top PRD. Four UJs could look heavy for one user, but here they are load-bearing: they are the scenarios (tired evening, setup, surprise question, catch-up) that the evening UX and the planning logic must satisfy. Multi-user support (FR-1, FR-2) is justified by §1 ("bygges for flere brukere") and kept minimal. Rigor is proportionate, and it is neither over- nor under-formalized.

## Mechanical notes
- The **[Bør]** tag (FR-7) is not defined in the §4 legend, which only defines [Okt] and [Nov]. FR-24's heading has no tag, and only its section heading says [Strekkmål]. Add both to the legend and tag FR-24.
- ID continuity: there are no gaps or duplicates. §9.1 covers every FR exactly once, with FR-10 and FR-20 split across versions as intended.
- Assumptions Index roundtrip: there are no inline `[ASSUMPTION]` tags and no index. This is consistent with §0's statement that all assumptions are confirmed.
- Open Q1 (IBE160 deadline unconfirmed) conflicts mildly with §0 and §7, which treat 22.11 as fixed. That is fine, but mark it as "planlagt" in §7 until confirmed.
- FR-20's example "for IBE430: 19 av 21" depends on the addendum. That is fine, but keep it framed as an example so the FR stays course-agnostic (FR-3 "uten endringer i koden").
- The quiz result is defined as "andelen riktige" (§3) but displayed as "8 av 10" (FR-14). This is consistent, just note that both representations are intended.
